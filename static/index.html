Straight answers

What homeowners ask us first.

The questions we hear at almost every first visit. All questions

How much do I pay up front?

A small deposit to book your start date. After that, every payment is tied to a finished stage of work that you can see with your own eyes. You never pay for work that has not been done.

Will I get a written quote?

Always. After we visit and understand the job, you get a fixed, written quote that lists what is included. If something changes, we agree on it in writing before we do it.

How long will my renovation take?

It depends on the size and requirements. When we visit you for your free estimate, we'll discuss the timeline in detail.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the size and the finishes you choose. Book a free estimate and we will give you an honest range on the spot, then a fixed written quote.

Do I need to move out during the work?

For one room or a bathroom, usually not. For a major renovation, it can be easier to stay elsewhere for part of it. We plan the work around your family, kids and pets.

What if I change my mind halfway?

Changes are welcome. Before any change, we tell you what it does to the price and the timeline, and we only go ahead once you agree in writing.
