How we do it

Done right, the first time.

A bathroom is small, but it hides a lot of risk behind the tile. We take it down to the studs when it needs it, waterproof it properly, and finish it so it stays beautiful for years.

  • Full tear-out down to the studs when needed
  • Waterproofing behind every wet wall
  • Walk-in showers, glass enclosures and soaker tubs
  • Vanities, toilets, sinks and taps
  • Floor and wall tile, heated floors
  • Lighting, fans and electrical

How long it takes. It depends on the size and requirements. When we visit you for your free estimate, we'll discuss the timeline in detail.

Straight answers

Bathrooms questions.

All questions

We only have one bathroom. What happens?

Tell us early. We plan the work so the toilet is out of action for as short a time as possible, and we can arrange a temporary option if needed.

Do you do heated floors?

Yes. Electric in-floor heat is a common add-on and is installed under the tile before it goes down.

How much do I pay up front?

A small deposit to book your start date. After that, every payment is tied to a finished stage of work that you can see with your own eyes. You never pay for work that has not been done.

Will I get a written quote?

Always. After we visit and understand the job, you get a fixed, written quote that lists what is included. If something changes, we agree on it in writing before we do it.

How long will my renovation take?

It depends on the size and requirements. When we visit you for your free estimate, we'll discuss the timeline in detail.
